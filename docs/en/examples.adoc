== Examples

Samples are part of the specification package `{schema-uri}.zip` (directory `samples`).

=== fcd-all-lorry-car-sample.xml – segment with a separated traffic flow

The publication contains information for one segment with a separated traffic flow: seven
`physicalQuantity` elements – traffic status for all vehicles and speed and travel time for all vehicles,
cars and lorries.

[source,xml]
----
include::{samplesdir}/fcd-all-lorry-car-sample.xml[tags=**]
----

Envelope and header::
Root `d2:payload` of type `roa:ElaboratedDataPublication` with the attributes `profileName`,
`profileVersion` and the extension identification `extensionName`, `extensionVersion`; `feedType` refers
to the data source; publication period 300 s (`periodDefault`), calculation time `timeDefault`.

Traffic status::
For all vehicles the speed is 38 km/h at a free-flow speed of 50 km/h (76 %), hence the status is
`freeFlow`. The NDIC extension gives the level of traffic `level2` according to the speed ratio and a queue
148 m long from 40 % to 90 % of the segment length. Lorries travel at 18 km/h, cars at 58 km/h.

Travel time::
For all vehicles 549 s compared with 259 s in free flow, hence a delay `travelTimeDelay` of 290 s.

Quality and number of vehicles::
Attributes `supplierCalculatedDataQuality` (in per cent) and `numberOfInputValuesUsed` (number of floating
vehicles, `9999` for statistical values) on each value.

Location::
All values refer to the predefined location `6D476A44-AA44-4947-AA4B-7C4DD79BB67E` version 0.

=== fcd-congestion.xml – segment with a queue

Real data of 30 September 2026: segment `TS02125T15781` with a queue at night. The publication contains five
`physicalQuantity` elements – traffic status for all vehicles, speed for all vehicles and cars and travel
time for all vehicles; for cars only the free-flow speed.

[source,xml]
----
include::{samplesdir}/fcd-congestion.xml[tags=**]
----

Traffic status::
A speed of 13 km/h at a free-flow speed of 90 km/h (14 %) corresponds to the status `queuing` and the level
of traffic `level5`. The queue is 1,784 m long and lies from 10.56 % to 59.01 % of the segment length.

Travel time::
916 s compared with 133 s in free flow, delay `travelTimeDelay` 783 s.

Location::
The values refer to the predefined location `TS02125T15781` version `260601` from the FCD location set; the
provider's own segment identifier (`source`) is not given because it is identical.
