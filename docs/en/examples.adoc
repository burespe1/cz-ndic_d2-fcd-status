== Message examples

Samples are part of the specification package `{schema-uri}.zip` (directory `samples`).

=== fcd-all-lorry-car-sample.xml – segment with a separated traffic flow

Real data of 30 September 2026: motorway segment `TS25575T01325` with a separated traffic flow at night.
The publication contains seven `physicalQuantity` elements – traffic status for all vehicles, speed for all
vehicles, cars and lorries, travel time for all vehicles and free-flow speed for cars and lorries.

[source,xml]
----
include::{samplesdir}/fcd-all-lorry-car-sample.xml[tags=**]
----

Envelope and header::
Root `d2:payload` of type `roa:ElaboratedDataPublication` with the attributes `@profileName`,
`@profileVersion` and the extension identification `@extensionName`, `@extensionVersion`; `feedType` refers
to the data source; publication period 300 s (`periodDefault`), calculation time `timeDefault`.

Traffic status::
For all vehicles the speed is 111 km/h at a free-flow speed of 130 km/h (85 %), hence the status is
`freeFlow` and the level of traffic in the NDIC extension `level1`; there is no queue. Cars travel at
110 km/h (free flow 130 km/h), lorries at 100 km/h (free flow 80 km/h).

Travel time::
For all vehicles 87 s compared with 74 s in free flow, hence a delay `travelTimeDelay` of 13 s.

Quality and number of vehicles::
Values for all vehicles carry `@supplierCalculatedDataQuality` (95 %) and `@numberOfInputValuesUsed`
(10 vehicles), speeds of cars and lorries only the number of vehicles (3 and 7); free-flow values carry no
quality attributes.

Location::
All values refer to the predefined location `TS25575T01325` version `260601` from the FCD location set (sample `predefined_location_set-fcd.xml` of the format `cz-ndic_d2-pls`).

=== fcd-congestion.xml – segment with a queue

Real data of 30 September 2026: segment `TS02125T15781` with a queue at night. The publication contains five
`physicalQuantity` elements – traffic status for all vehicles, speed for all vehicles and cars and travel
time for all vehicles; for cars only the free-flow speed.

[source,xml]
----
include::{samplesdir}/fcd-congestion.xml[tags=**]
----

Traffic status::
A speed of 13 km/h at a free-flow speed of 90 km/h (14 %) corresponds to the status `queuing` and the level
of traffic `level5`. The queue is 1,784 m long and lies from 10.56 % to 59.01 % of the segment length.

Travel time::
916 s compared with 133 s in free flow, delay `travelTimeDelay` 783 s.

Location::
The values refer to the predefined location `TS02125T15781` version `260601` from the FCD location set.
